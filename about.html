Our Story

North Star Bakery began as a neighborhood bakery built around a simple idea: fresh food and friendly service can bring people together. We make everyday breads and pastries as well as cakes for important family and community celebrations.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We look for quality ingredients and work with suppliers that fit our focus on fresh, handmade products. We aim to keep our menu approachable while paying attention to the ingredients that go into each bake.

Meet the Team

Our bakers and staff work together to prepare products, help customers, and keep the bakery welcoming. We value careful preparation, friendly service, and pride in the food we make.
